# FAQ

## Why did my order notification not arrive?

A notification may not arrive when the feature is disabled, the destination address is incorrect, or the destination does not accept the request.

## Does ResolveLab try again automatically?

ResolveLab records a failed attempt in Recent activity. Automatic retry depends on the notification type. If no retry appears, follow the recovery steps or contact support.

## Is my order data lost when a notification fails?

No. The order remains in ResolveLab. The failure only describes delivery of the notification.
