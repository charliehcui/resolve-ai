# Engineer escalation checklist

Escalate when required source records are missing, outside the problem time, or contradictory. Preserve the customer handoff, valid evidence references, unanswered questions, and suggested next checks.

Do not ask the customer to repeat confirmed information. Do not convert an unsupported possibility into a confirmed cause.
